Parts

Click a part to drop it, then drag it into place. Click a pin, then another pin, to wire them.

Selected

Select a part on the board to edit it. Press R to rotate, Delete to remove.

Ask your agent something like:

  • “Build a circuit that lights a red LED from a 9V battery, safely.”
  • “Why is my LED not lighting up?” (after you wire one)
  • “Add a switch so I can turn the LED on and off.”
  • “What’s the current through the resistor right now?”
